# KeyFlow 0.4.0 — Single-Slide Visual Intelligence Evaluation Rubric

## 8 Test Archetypes

| Archetype | Input Nature | Primary Capability Tested |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Core Thesis (观点表达)** | A single strong argument or insight | Visual creativity & typographic/conceptual punch |
| **2. Causal Logic (因果关系)** | Business or technical cause-and-effect chain | Relationship & flow visualization |
| **3. Comparison (对比分析)** | Two competing models, architectures, or eras | Contrast composition & visual tension |
| **4. Data Storytelling (数据表达)** | Statistical evidence & metrics | Chart selection, hierarchy, & takeaway clarity |
| **5. Complex System (复杂系统)** | Multiple interdependent components | Spatial organization & grouping without clutter |
| **6. Temporal Evolution (时间演变)** | Historical progression or roadmap | Timeline & state-change expression |
| **7. Business Story (商业故事)** | A real-world event with stakes and turning point | Visual hero & dramatic tension |
| **8. Abstract Concept (抽象概念)** | A hard-to-visualize philosophical or technical idea | Visual metaphor & originality |

## 5 Evaluation Dimensions (1–5 scale)

1. **Content Fidelity (内容忠实度)**: Zero distortion, omission of key facts, or fabricated claims.
2. **Visual Fit (视觉适配度)**: The chosen visual form is the most natural, illuminating way to express *this* specific content.
3. **Design Craft (设计完成度)**: Professional composition, typography, color harmony, alignment, contrast, and intentional whitespace.
4. **Visual Diversity (视觉多样性)**: Across different archetypes, the Agent genuinely adopts different visual languages rather than falling back to `title + 3 cards + icon`.
5. **Communication Efficiency (表达效率)**: A viewer grasps the core message within 3 seconds without reading dense prose.
